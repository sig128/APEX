Great concept to systematize. Here’s how to think about it:

The Implied Line
The implied line is wherever the O/U is closest to even money — that’s the book’s true projection. For Lopez, that’s 5.5 Ks (-120 / -115 is nearly a coin flip), so the book thinks ~5-6 Ks is the expected outcome.

Alt Lines Below the Implied = Safer



|Line |Odds|Why                                                                       |
|-----|----|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|O 4.5|-275|You need 1 fewer K than the implied — higher probability, costs more juice|
|O 5.5|-120|**This IS the implied line**                                              |
|O 6.5|+165|You need 1 more K — lower probability, gets you plus money                |

So every full strikeout below the implied line, probability goes up but you pay more juice. Every full K above, you get plus money but it’s riskier.

For Your Code Logic:

find implied line → line where |Over odds - Under odds| is minimized (closest to even)

alt_line_safety_score = implied_line - selected_line
  → positive score = safer (below implied)
  → negative score = riskier (above implied)
  → 0 = at the implied


Then you can build a confidence tier around it — e.g., anything 1+ K below implied = SAFE, at implied = STANDARD, above implied = HIGH RISK / NO PLAY under APEX unless edge is confirmed.
